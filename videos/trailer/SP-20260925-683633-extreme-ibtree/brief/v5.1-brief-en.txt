ORBACE SU-PU REPLAY TRAILER — V5.1
Title: "One Contradiction Changed Everything"

EDITORIAL RULE (governs every decision)
Reveal the turning point. Never reveal the entire story.
The replay is the product. The trailer is the door.
Open on the best moment. Leave the ending for the site.
End on an invitation to the replay, not on a tease of the ending.

OUTPUT
- Primary: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, exactly 48.0 seconds.
- Secondary: 16:9, 1920x1080, cropped from the same master. Do not regenerate.
- Source: Orbace Su-Pu replay SP-20260925-683633.
- CTA redirect orbacesudoku.com/one MUST resolve to SP-20260925-683633,
  not to the library page. Confirm before delivery.

SOURCE FIDELITY (hard rules)
- Genuine screen-recorded Su-Pu replay footage only.
- Preserve real replay timing, order, chapter titles, branch visualization,
  timeline, and controls exactly as rendered.
- Allowed: freeze frames, speed changes, scrubbing, cropping, slow push
  (camera only, never UI motion), holding longer on a real chapter title.
- Forbidden: invented moves, fabricated solver states, artificial branches,
  redrawn overlays, invented UI, invented chapter names, invented coordinates.
- If a beat has no matching replay footage, delete the beat. Never fabricate.

SAFE AREA
All critical elements stay inside the center 60% width of the 9:16 frame:
contradiction node, branch, chapter title, progress bar, captions, text cards.

CAPTIONS
- Burn captions for every narration sentence.
- Clean sans-serif, white, subtle shadow, max two lines, upper-middle third.
- Width never exceeds 60% of frame.
- Soft CTA layer is separate from narration captions and can be toggled per
  aspect ratio without re-exporting scenes.

CHAPTER TITLE RULE (new in V5.1)
Every time a real Su-Pu chapter title appears on screen, hold on it 0.5 seconds
longer than natural before cutting away. Do not slow the replay playback to do
this. Let the title linger into the next shot. This is the only place the
trailer explicitly sells the idea that a solve is organized into chapters.

AUDIO
Voice: warm, thoughtful documentary narrator, original synthetic voice.
Approx 130 wpm. Never imitate or resemble any identifiable person.
Music: minimal ambient, 60-70 BPM, no percussion, no risers, no impacts.

SILENCE RULE
At the contradiction: cut music, room tone, ambience.
Hold 1.0 second of true silence with the contradiction marker visible.
Then one VO line. Then 1.0 second before music returns.
Total quiet window approximately 2.5 seconds. Not a stall — a held beat.
During silence, keep a very slow 3% push on the frame. No UI motion.

TIMING (48 seconds, fixed)
1. 0:00-0:02  Cold open — contradiction frame
2. 0:02-0:05  Rewind
3. 0:05-0:10  Chapter One, accelerated, title held
4. 0:10-0:16  Chapter Two, branch growth, title held
5. 0:16-0:22  Approach to contradiction
6. 0:22-0:24  Silence, held
7. 0:24-0:30  Resume, bridge line, soft CTA
8. 0:30-0:37  Endgame at 2x, freeze at ~60%
9. 0:37-0:48  End card — invitation, not tease

SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN (0:00-0:02)
Open on the real contradiction frame from the replay. No explanation.
Caption only: "One contradiction solved this."
No music for the first 0.5 second. Then music enters quietly.
No zoom, no push. Let the frame land.

SCENE 2 — REWIND (0:02-0:05)
Real replay rewind. Scrubbing only. No effects.
Real replay timeline visible.
VO: "This puzzle looked impossible."

SCENE 3 — CHAPTER ONE (0:05-0:10)
Play Chapter One at 3x. Real chapter title visible, not covered.
Hold on the chapter title 0.5 seconds longer than natural before cutting.
VO: "Then everything stopped."

SCENE 4 — CHAPTER TWO, BRANCH GROWTH (0:10-0:16)
Enter Chapter Two. Show the real chapter title. Hold it 0.5 seconds longer
than natural.
Then play at 1x: the chosen cell, the branch beginning to grow, the branch
extending toward the contradiction. Use the real branch visualization.
Do not add overlays. Do not label anything.
VO: "One branch. One question."

SCENE 5 — APPROACH (0:16-0:22)
Continue at 1x as the branch reaches the contradiction.
VO: "And then the puzzle answered back."

SCENE 6 — SILENCE (0:22-0:24)
Freeze on the contradiction. Contradiction marker fully visible.
Cut all audio. Hold 1.0 second of silence.
Slow 3% camera push only. No UI motion.
VO (after silence): "That path was impossible."

SCENE 7 — RESUME (0:24-0:30)
Replay resumes. Speed 2x.
Soft CTA layer appears (lower third, small, inside safe area):
"Full replay ↓ below"

VO (bridge line, new in V5.1): "Now we can replay the exact moment the
puzzle changed."
(pause 0.8s)
VO: "From that single proof... the rest unfolds."
Note: the bridge line replaces the earlier "From that single proof" opening
pause. Do not repeat the phrase "everything changed."

SCENE 8 — ENDGAME, DO NOT FINISH (0:30-0:37)
Play endgame at 2x. Do not show the solved board. Do not show the final
chapter in full.
Freeze when the real replay progress bar reads approximately 60%.
No VO during the freeze.
On-screen caption, new in V5.1: "Watch the complete replay"

SCENE 9 — END CARD (0:37-0:48)
Dissolve from the frozen ~60% frame to a minimal Orbace end card.
No feature list. No screenshots. No app UI. No solved board.
Centered, inside 60% width:
"Watch the complete replay"
"orbacesudoku.com/one"
"Replay the thinking."
VO: "The full replay is linked in the description."
Hold 0.5 seconds after the last line.

PACING
Never rush. Long pauses are intentional. Silence is a held beat, not a stall.
Chapter title holds are deliberate, not accidental.

CAMERA
No shake. No whip pans. Max one slow push per scene.
During the silence, only the 3% push is allowed.

COLOR
Do not stylize. Respect the real Su-Pu interface.
No neon, no cinematic grading, no exaggerated contrast.

NEGATIVE

replay states.
Do not explain IB Tree.

Do not add fake branch graphics.
Do not over-edit.
Do not interrupt the replay unnecessarily.
Do not render statistics as UI panels.

Do not show the solved board.
Do not use risers, whooshes, or impacts.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
Viewers should leave wanting to know:
- What happened after the contradiction?
- Why did that branch fail?
- What are the other two chapters?
- How did the puzzle finish?
If the trailer answers all four, it has revealed too much.
The trailer must not answer the fourth.
The trailer must end by inviting the viewer to the replay, not by teasing
the ending.
