## EDITORIAL RULE (governs every decision)
Reveal the turning point. Never reveal the entire story.
The replay is the product. The trailer is the door.
The technique is the story inside the replay, not the subject of the ad.
The hook describes what the viewer experiences, not what the puzzle experiences.
End on an invitation to the replay, not a tease of the ending.
