## V1 to V2 changes

- Preserved all six validated Sudoku states and the established logical sequence.
- Reduced on-screen prose to short semantic labels; narration explains why while the board shows what changes.
- Retimed the fork, held-branch, parallel-seven, completion, and circular-verification beats to the spoken phrases.
- Added restrained selection, chain, fixed-seven, verification, VERIFIED, and seal accents.
- Added optional English captions with sentence-level timing and a non-social, documentary presentation.
- Added a genuinely muted master with no audio stream.

The side-by-side review confirms lower cognitive load in V2: the board remains the dominant visual, labels no longer compete with narration, and the verification turn receives a distinct pause. No generative imagery or generated Sudoku content was used.
