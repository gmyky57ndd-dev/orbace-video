## Working title

**Where Does the 9 Go? A Failed Trial Proves It**

Short title:

**Which 9 Survives?**

## Creative intent

Make the viewer participate in one clear decision, then watch the recorded consequences prove the answer. The video should feel calm and intelligent rather than like a rewind puzzle. Every major scene keeps the full 9×9 grid visible; Box 4 and Box 3 are emphasized through focus treatment or a contextual inset, never by showing an isolated partial grid as the only board.

## Voice direction

- Calm, confident, and curious.
- Make the two coordinates distinct and unhurried.
- A slight pause after “Let’s test the lower position.”
- Slow down on “Box Three has no place for six.”
- Record the final CTA with the same narrator, microphone, room tone, and processing as the opening.
- Do not insert a second voice or separate middle-video announcer.
- Do not use “rewind,” “back,” or other reverse-order language.
