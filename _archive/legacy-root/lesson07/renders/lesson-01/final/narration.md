# Lesson 01 narration — Two Homes for a Nine

Two homes. One nine. Which one breaks the puzzle?

This Extreme grid resists the usual opening moves. So look at its structure.

In Box 1, nine has exactly two homes: R1C1 or R2C3. One of them must be true.

Assume R2C3 is nine, then follow every forced placement.

After only five steps, the branch forces two nines into the same row. Contradiction.

So R2C3 cannot be nine. The other home is proven: R1C1 equals nine.

That single placement opens the grid. Basic techniques can carry the solve forward.

That is ibtree: find a binary fork, trace one branch, and let proof—not guessing—decide.
