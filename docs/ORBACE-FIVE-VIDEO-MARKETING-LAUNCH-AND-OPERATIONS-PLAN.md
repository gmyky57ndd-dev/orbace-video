# Orbace Sudoku Five-Video Marketing Launch and Operating Plan

**Review date:** August 24, 2026  
**Scope:** Journal Lesson 1, Journal Lesson 7, Replay V2, Solve Record/Scorecard V1, and Brand Film V1  
**Purpose:** turn the five completed creative concepts into a controlled launch sequence and a sustainable content operation.

## Executive decision

The five videos form a strong launch set, but the current files are **review masters, not a fully release-ready distribution package**.

Use them as one funnel:

1. **Brand Film** creates the category story.
2. **Replay** supplies the clearest product hook.
3. **Solve Record** supplies product proof and differentiation.
4. **Lesson 1** demonstrates that a saved solve can become understanding.
5. **Lesson 7** establishes depth, authority, and an expandable Journal series.

Do not publish all five at once. Complete the release fixes, then stagger the films across approximately two weeks. After launch, use Replay/Scorecard clips weekly, Journal lessons biweekly or monthly, and the Brand Film only for major campaign moments.

## 2. What works across the full set

The videos now share a recognizable Orbace grammar:

- deep green and warm paper surfaces;
- editorial serif propositions and functional sans-serif labels;
- crimson seal and restrained gold rules;
- real interface evidence rather than invented product imagery;
- calm pacing and deliberate motion;
- a consistent promise that a solve can be played, preserved, replayed, studied, and carried into competition.

This is a meaningful competitive advantage. Most Sudoku marketing shows only a grid, difficulty, or relaxation. This library can show a connected intellectual journey.

## 3. Pre-launch release gates

Do not schedule public posts or paid media until these items pass.

### Gate A — repair audio

1. **Lesson 7:** restore the approved V4 voice/music mix. The inspected final measures approximately -64.8 LUFS with a -57.2 dBTP peak, which is effectively inaudible.
2. **Replay:** add the approved restrained sound system or deliberately publish a truly silent file without a misleading empty audio stream. Preferred release: subtle taps, rewind texture, turning-point reduction, and end resolve.
3. **Solve Record:** add restrained scorecard/Replay sound and an end resolve.
4. **Brand Film:** replace temporary narration. Ensure the audio stream spans the full 30 seconds; the current voiced and captioned files contain only 16 seconds of audio.
5. Measure final social masters near -14 LUFS integrated with true peak no higher than -1 dBTP, unless a platform-specific master has a documented reason to differ.

### Gate B — render distribution-quality masters

The current video bitrates are approximately:

- Lesson 1: 1.78 Mbps;
- Lesson 7: 0.33 Mbps;
- Replay: 0.16 Mbps;
- Solve Record: 0.46 Mbps;
- Brand Film: 0.25 Mbps.

These are efficient review encodes but too low for archival upload masters. Re-render from Remotion/source assets rather than transcoding the compressed review files.

Create:

- H.264 High Profile;
- progressive scan;
- 4:2:0;
- BT.709/Rec.709 color metadata;
- AAC 48 kHz stereo;
- fast-start MP4;
- approximately 8–12 Mbps for 1080p upload masters, with visually lossless mezzanine masters retained separately.

YouTube's current reference recommendation for ordinary 1080p SDR uploads is 10 Mbps, with the source frame rate preserved.

### Gate C — product and continuity approval

- Confirm the Replay claims match the visible moves.
- Confirm the Scorecard and Replay combination is presented as two truthful examples, not as proof that the shown Scorecard produced that separate 63-step Replay.
- Confirm all Learn, competition, score, Su-Pu, and sharing language matches the current product.
- Remove or crop nonessential status-bar artifacts where this can be done without misrepresenting the UI.
- Confirm the current direct App Store and Google Play destinations.

### Gate D — end cards and captions

- Apply the shared two-destination end-card system to Lesson 7.
- Preserve relevant page + download URL on Replay and Solve Record.
- Brand Film should show `orbacesudoku.com` and `orbacesudoku.com/download`.
- Produce clean SRT files for every narrated video.
- Burn captions into short-form social versions; retain clean and SRT versions for YouTube accessibility and localization.

## 4. Channel jobs

### YouTube — primary content library

YouTube should be the permanent home for all five assets. Vertical or square videos up to three minutes are categorized as Shorts, so both 60-second Journal films and the vertical feature films can operate as Shorts. The 16:9 Brand Film should be a standard channel video.

Create these playlists:

1. **Start Here — Orbace Sudoku**
2. **Replay & Su-Pu**
3. **Orbace Sudoku Journal**
4. **How Orbace Works**

Channel layout:

- channel trailer for non-subscribers: Brand Film;
- featured video for returning viewers: newest Journal lesson;
- first shelf: Start Here;
- second shelf: Journal;
- third shelf: Replay & Su-Pu.

Use custom thumbnails for the Brand Film and any later long-form versions. YouTube currently supports title/thumbnail A/B testing for eligible long-form videos, but not Shorts. Optimize Shorts using opening-frame tests and retention, not thumbnail A/B testing.

### Instagram and Facebook — reach and product proof

Use native 9:16 versions with audio and all key text inside the Reels safe zone. Meta explicitly recommends 9:16 Reels creative with quality audio and safe-zone placement.

Best assets:

- Replay as the primary organic and paid Reel;
- Solve Record as the retargeting Reel;
- a new 9:16 Brand Film derivative for launch week;
- 20–35 second Journal extracts, not the full lesson every time;
- carousel companions using the Journal's key reasoning frames.

Do not rely on automatic platform reframing of the 16:9 Brand Film. Produce an authored vertical version.

### TikTok — experimental discovery, not a simple repost channel

Replay is the best initial TikTok asset, but the polished editorial cut should be tested against a more native derivative:

- founder/player opening line in the first 1–2 seconds;
- real full-screen 9:16 product evidence;
- sound on;
- proposition inside the first three seconds;
- direct CTA at the end.

TikTok's current business guidance prioritizes native 9:16, audio, a clear hook in the first six seconds, and the proposition in the first three. Treat the five polished films as source material for tests, not the entire TikTok operation.

### Website — conversion and product explanation

- Homepage: Brand Film poster with click-to-play; do not autoplay the voiced 30-second film.
- Homepage lightweight motion: create a separate muted 6–10 second loop from Brand Film/Replay.
- `/su-pu`: embed Replay first, Solve Record second.
- Scorecard section: embed Solve Record.
- `/journal`: use the Journal playlist and individual lesson embeds.
- `/download`: use a short Replay/Brand derivative near store buttons.

Every embed should have a static poster, meaningful alt/context text, lazy loading, and a direct download CTA nearby.

### Email and lifecycle messaging

Do not place five videos into one email.

- Launch announcement: Brand Film thumbnail linked to the website.
- Product education email: Replay.
- Follow-up/activation email: Solve Record.
- Continuing education newsletter: alternate Journal lessons.
- Inactive-user re-engagement: a new Tea Moment or Replay clip, not the Brand Film repeatedly.

Use a thumbnail with a play symbol and link to a hosted page; avoid heavy embedded video in email.

### App stores

The existing films are marketing masters, not automatically compliant app-preview assets. Create dedicated store-preview cutdowns only after reviewing current Apple and Google requirements. Replay should be the first candidate because it communicates a unique product behavior quickly.

### LinkedIn and press/founder channels

Use the 16:9 Brand Film once, paired with a founder explanation of why Sudoku deserves a solve record. Use Journal content selectively as evidence of product depth. LinkedIn is not the main consumer-acquisition engine; its role is company narrative, recruiting, partnerships, and credibility.

Use a relative schedule anchored to **Launch Day (D0)**. Do not assign calendar dates until the four release gates pass.

## 7. Regular operating cadence after launch

The five films are a seed library. They should become a repeatable system rather than five frequently reposted ads.

### Minimum sustainable cadence

Every week:

- one 12–25 second Tea Moment, Replay, turning-point, or Scorecard vertical clip;
- one supporting still, carousel, poll, or puzzle question;
- one community response session using comments as research.

Every two weeks:

- one Journal-derived short or reasoning explainer;
- one email/lifecycle placement tied to a relevant user stage.

Every month:

- one complete new Journal lesson video or high-value technique study;
- one new acquisition hook derived from real current UI;
- one performance and creative-fatigue review.

Every quarter or major release:

- refresh the Brand Film or its product scenes only if the product/story changed;
- recapture current UI;
- revalidate end-card URLs and store destinations;
- archive outdated creative rather than continuing to circulate obsolete screens.

### Recommended monthly publishing rhythm

| Week | YouTube | Instagram/Facebook | TikTok | Website/email |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New Replay/feature Short | Same core idea, platform-safe version | Native hook test | Product-page embed or update |
| 2 | Journal excerpt | Reasoning carousel/Reel | Puzzle question or response | Journal newsletter |
| 3 | Scorecard/Su-Pu proof Short | Retargeting Reel | Founder/player explanation | Activation email |
| 4 | Full Journal lesson or compilation | Best lesson excerpt | Winning-format iteration | Monthly learning roundup |

## 8. Content production pipeline

For every new canonical solve, capture one connected source package:

1. Tea Moment or Puzzle Pack play;
2. candidate-note moment;
3. correction or decision;
4. completion;
5. Scorecard;
6. Su-Pu save/Record Hall;
7. Replay turning point;
8. related Learn/Journal evidence if genuinely applicable.

From that package, produce:

- one 15-second acquisition clip;
- one 15–20 second Scorecard/record clip;
- one 30–60 second teaching clip;
- three stills/carousel frames;
- one email thumbnail;
- one website poster;
- one creator/founder briefing note.

This avoids the current continuity problem of combining different solves and makes campaign attribution easier.

## 9. Measurement framework

### Awareness

- reach and unique viewers;
- YouTube engaged views, not only raw Shorts starts;
- viewed-versus-swiped rate;
- average percentage viewed;
- completion and rewatch rate;
- branded search and direct traffic.

### Consideration

- profile/channel visits;
- clicks to `/su-pu`, `/journal`, and `/download`;
- playlist continuation;
- Journal-page engaged time;
- store-button click-through.

### Acquisition

- store-page visits by channel/video;
- install conversion rate;
- cost per qualified store visit and install;
- first Tea Moment started;
- first puzzle completed.

### Activation and product proof

- Scorecard viewed after completion;
- Replay opened;
- Su-Pu saved;
- second puzzle started;
- D1 and D7 retention.

### Decision rules

- Do not judge Shorts by raw views alone because YouTube counts starts/replays in the public Shorts view metric; use Engaged Views and retention.
- Promote a creative when it produces qualified clicks, installs, or activation—not merely cheap impressions.
- If the first-three-second hold is weak, test the opening, not the whole brand system.
- If hold is strong but clicks are weak, test CTA and landing-page match.
- If clicks are strong but installs are weak, investigate the store listing before replacing the video.
- If installs are strong but first Tea Moment/Replay use is weak, investigate onboarding and product expectation match.

## 10. Tracking and links

Use one canonical destination per post. Add channel/video/version UTMs, for example:

```text
utm_source=youtube
utm_medium=organic_short
utm_campaign=orbace_launch_2026
utm_content=replay_v2_v01
```

Keep a launch ledger with:

- publish date and time;
- platform and post URL;
- exact file/version;
- title, caption, thumbnail, CTA, and destination;
- paid/organic status and spend;
- 24-hour, 7-day, and 28-day metrics;
- decision and next iteration.

Never reuse one undifferentiated download link across every channel if attribution can be preserved.

## 12. Final recommendation

Launch with **Brand Film → Replay → Solve Record**, supported by the two Journal lessons as proof of depth. Make YouTube the permanent organized library, Meta the primary short-form reach/retargeting layer, TikTok an experimental native-creative channel, the website the conversion layer, and email the activation/retention layer.

The most important operational change is to capture each future solve end-to-end and generate a family of marketing assets from it. That will keep product truth, campaign continuity, and production efficiency aligned as Orbace grows.
