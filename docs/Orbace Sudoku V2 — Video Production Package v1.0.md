# Orbace Sudoku V2 — Video Production Package v1.0

**Production master · August 20, 2026**

## 1. Creative system

The three launch videos form one narrative system:

**V1 — REPLAY**  
*I can replay my thinking.*

**V2 — SCORECARD / SOLVE RECORD**  
*Orbace preserves how I solved.*

**V3 — BRAND FILM**  
*Play → Replay → Understand → Keep → Compete.*

### Campaign creative spine

Use **one canonical demonstration solve** across V1, V2 and V3.

The canonical solve should:

- be Hard tier;
- contain approximately 45–70 recorded steps;
- visibly use candidate notes;
- contain one clear turning point;
- produce 3–5 consequential moves immediately after that turning point;
- have a Scorecard with one meaningful penalty and at least one positive bonus;
- contain no test/admin identities;
- be associated with a seeded production-quality demo account.

Example narrative:

> Step 24 resolves a candidate pair → one candidate is eliminated → decisive digit becomes available → several subsequent placements open.

Do not select the solve based on numerical score. Select it based on **visual legibility of the reasoning**.

---

# 2. Global production rules

## 2.1 Product truth

Every visible Orbace interface must come from a real production-equivalent build.

Never generate:

- Sudoku grids;
- candidate notes;
- Orbace UI;
- Scorecards;
- replay screens;
- competition screens;
- Orbace logos;
- store badges;
- product text.

AI-generated footage may show humans, rooms, tea, phones with the screen **face-down or invisible**, and other environmental imagery.

---

## 2.2 Capture master

Capture product footage at:

**Resolution:** native device resolution  
**Frame rate:** 60 fps where possible  
**Orientation:** portrait for V1/V2  
**Codec:** highest-quality native screen recording available  
**UI scaling:** default production scaling  
**Brightness:** approximately 70–80%  
**Appearance:** production/default Orbace appearance

Do not screen-record through a browser simulator if native mobile capture is available.

Record each required interaction **2–3 times**.

Keep raw recordings untrimmed.

---

## 2.3 Device preparation

Before capture:

- enable Do Not Disturb;
- disable notification previews;
- disable low-power mode;
- use full/healthy battery state;
- remove VPN/status anomalies;
- ensure correct local time;
- remove debugging overlays;
- remove developer indicators;
- remove accessibility inspection indicators;
- use a seeded production-quality account;
- verify no `admin`, `appreview`, `TestPlayer`, `SwiftCell`, UUID/debug identifiers or placeholder data appears.

For competition screens, confirm displayed time-zone language matches the production V8.1 UX.

All three videos should share one motion language.

Use Orbace's production brand typefaces/tokens.

**Narrative overlays:** medium/semibold

### Maximum text

Narrative overlay:

**≤8 words preferred.**

---

## 21.1 Position

For vertical:

Keep text within approximately:

**90 px horizontal safe margin**  
**180 px top/bottom platform-safe margin**

But move copy where necessary to avoid important UI.

For 16:9 brand film:

Compose important subjects and product footage inside a **center-safe 9:16 region** wherever possible.

This substantially improves derivative production.

---

## 21.2 Motion

Default text entrance:

**6–10 frame opacity + 6–12 px movement.**

Default exit:

**6–8 frame opacity.**

Do not use:

- bouncing;
- elastic motion;
- kinetic word-by-word typography;
- spinning;
- dramatic zoom typography;
- TikTok-style auto-caption highlighting.

Orbace motion should feel **deliberate and editorial**.

# 22. Transition language

Use only four principal transition types:

### 1. Hard cut

For clarity and product interaction.

### 2. Short dissolve

For environmental/time transition.

### 3. Match cut

Preferred for:

**Replay → Learn**

**physical phone → product screen.**

### 4. Controlled digital crop

For drawing attention to turning points or Scorecard evidence.

Avoid transition packs.

# 32. Review gates

## Gate A — product correctness

Product/engineering reviews every visible screen.

Confirm:

- correct terminology;
- correct scoring;
- correct UI;
- correct replay behavior;
- correct competition type;
- no obsolete Monthly terminology where Grand is intended;
- no test data;
- no live solution exposure.

## Gate B — comprehension

Show V1 without explanation to at least five people.

Ask only:

> “What does this app do that seemed different?”

Pass if the dominant unaided response is equivalent to:

> “It records/replays how you solved the Sudoku.”

For V2:

> “What did that card tell you?”

Desired answer:

> “How the solve went/how I solved.”

Do not ask whether they “liked” it until after comprehension testing.

## Gate C — brand

Confirm:

- calm rather than sleepy;
- intelligent rather than academic;
- premium rather than luxurious;
- competitive without esports/racing cues;
- tea supports the ritual rather than becoming a tea advertisement.
