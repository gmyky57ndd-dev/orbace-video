# Su-Pu Trailer Distribution Plan

Sep 26, 2026 · @Justin Zhang

## Summary

Put the four trailers on free, owned and community channels first, then spend on ads only behind the video and audience that win in week 1. YouTube and your own site are the top two because they cost nothing and give the most clickable links. Paid ads rank fourth: they are predictable but only pay off once you know which trailer converts.

ROI here means expected su-pu page visits per hour or dollar spent. Rankings are judgment calls, not measured results; week 1 data should re-rank them.

## Channel playbook (in ROI order)

### 1. YouTube organic

- **What:** upload all four. Post the 9:16 cuts as Shorts, and the 16:9 cuts as regular videos, where description links are clickable.
- **Link:** set each Short's *related video* to its 16:9 twin. Put the su-pu link in the first description line and in a pinned comment on every upload.
- **Titles:** "This easy Sudoku hides one move most people miss" (Easy) · "One contradiction cracked this extreme Sudoku" (Extreme).
- **Cadence:** Easy Short on day 1, Extreme Short on day 3, the 16:9 uploads on days 1 and 3, then one new su-pu trailer a week.
- **KPI:** su-pu visits from YouTube per 1,000 views; target 5+.

### 2. Your site, email and in-app

- **What:** embed Easy 16:9 on the homepage and Learn page, and Extreme 16:9 on the Su-Pu library. Email existing users ("Two replays worth watching"). Add an in-app card linking to both.
- **Downloads:** below each embed, add "Save your own solves as su-pus — get the app." This is the highest-intent moment for the secondary goal.
- **Google Play:** add Easy 16:9 as the store's promo video (it takes a YouTube link).
- **KPI:** replay starts from embeds, and store clicks per embed view.

### 3. Pinterest video pins

- **What:** Easy 9:16 as a video pin linking straight to the su-pu page. Pin it to boards like "Sudoku tips" and "Brain games".
- **Copy:** "You'd call this puzzle easy. Would you have seen this move? Watch the full solve."
- **Cadence:** one pin now, then re-pin to a second board in week 2. Pins keep working for months.
- **KPI:** outbound clicks.

### 4. YouTube paid (Demand Gen)

- **When:** start on day 8, only behind the week-1 winner (highest su-pu visits per 1,000 views).
- **Setup:** three campaigns totalling $20/day — $10.50 for the week-1 winner, $4.50 for the other trailer, $5 for Extreme on Sudoku channels. Full steps are in the last section.
- **Stop rule:** pause any ad whose CTR is under 0.8% after 1,000 impressions, or whose cost per replay start is over $1.

### 5. Facebook groups and Reels

- **What:** Easy 9:16 as a Reel on the Orbace page. In groups, post as a helpful lesson ("here's a move beginners miss"), with the video native and the link in a comment only where group rules allow.
- **KPI:** link clicks and comments.

### 6. Maker communities

- **What:** Extreme 16:9 embedded in an r/SideProject post and a Show HN post ("We built move-by-move replays for Sudoku solves"), linking to the Extreme su-pu. Stay in the thread for the first 3 hours.
- **KPI:** a one-day visit spike, plus signups.

### 7. Instagram Reels / TikTok

- **What:** Easy 9:16 first; Extreme 9:16 in week 2 if Easy holds attention. Put the link in bio and say "link in bio" in the caption.
- **KPI:** 3-second hold rate and bio-link clicks. Drop the channel if bio clicks stay under 0.2% of views.

### 8. Creator and teacher outreach

- **What:** send the Extreme 16:9 to 10–20 Sudoku YouTubers and math teachers, offering featured su-pus of their own solves (template in the campaign doc).
- **KPI:** replies and any feature.

### 9. r/orbace\_sudoku (own community)

- **What:** post all four trailers with su-pu links, one new replay a week, and a pinned "Share your su-pu" thread. Nothing goes to r/sudoku; it is not worth the rule risk.
- **KPI:** members, and su-pu visits from utm\_source=reddit\_orbace.

### 10. LinkedIn / X

- **What:** Extreme 16:9 with its QR code, framed as a product story from you as founder.
- **KPI:** clicks; low priority.

## Tracking and scale/stop rules

Judge every channel on su-pu replay starts, not views. That is the number the primary goal depends on.

| Metric | Where | Scale if | Stop if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Su-pu visits per 1,000 views | GA4, by utm\_source/utm\_content | 5+ | under 1 after 2,000 views |
| Replay start rate on landing | GA4 event on the su-pu page | 60%+ | under 30% (fix the page, not the ad) |
| Cost per replay start (paid) | Google Ads + GA4 | under $0.50 | over $1.00 |
| Store clicks per su-pu visit | GA4 outbound click | 3%+ | under 1% (add the "save your own su-pu" prompt) |
| Easy vs Extreme | All channels | Shift budget to the one with the higher visits per view | — |

Before day 1, add the replay-start and store-click events to the su-pu pages; without them, the paid decision on day 8 is a guess.

## YouTube paid setup, step by step

Three campaigns, $20/day in total, starting day 8 (Mon Oct 5). Each trailer gets its own campaign so you control the split; inside a campaign, Google chooses between the 9:16 and 16:9 files.

| Campaign | Type | Videos | Budget/day | Bidding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — week-1 winner | Demand Gen | Winner's 9:16 + 16:9 | $10.50 | Maximize clicks |
| B — other trailer | Demand Gen | Other 9:16 + 16:9 | $4.50 | Maximize clicks |
| C — Extreme on Sudoku channels | Video (video views) | Extreme 16:9 | $5.00 | Target CPV, max $0.05 |

If week 1 is a tie, make Easy campaign A: it has the larger audience.

### Before you start (one-time)

1. **Upload all four videos to YouTube.** They can be the same public uploads used for organic posts. Ads can only use videos hosted on YouTube.
2. **Link your YouTube channel to Google Ads:** Tools → Data manager → YouTube.
3. **Add a `replay_start` event.** Your site already loads GA4 (tag G-0RWLZBGC6Y). Have the web team fire `replay_start` when the Play button is first pressed on a su-pu page, and `store_click` on the app-store badges. Mark both as key events in GA4.
4. **Import the key events into Google Ads:** Goals → Conversions → New → Import → Google Analytics 4. Set `replay_start` as the primary conversion and `store_click` as secondary. Demand Gen requires conversion tracking even when bidding for clicks.

### Campaign A and B (Demand Gen) — same steps, different budget and video

1. **New campaign** → objective **Website traffic** → campaign type **Demand Gen**. Name it `SuPu-A-Easy` (or `-Extreme`).
2. **Goal:** Clicks. **Bidding:** Maximize clicks. After 30+ recorded `replay_start` conversions, switch to Maximize conversions on `replay_start`.
3. **Budget:** Daily. $10.50 for A, $4.50 for B. Google may warn the budget is low; that is fine for a test.
4. **Locations:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia. **Language:** English.
5. **Ad group → Channels:** choose *Let me choose* → YouTube only (in-stream, in-feed and Shorts). Turn off Discover, Gmail, Maps and Display; they bring cheap clicks from people who never press Play.
6. **Ad group → Audience:** create a custom segment of people who searched for the phrases below. Leave optimized targeting off for week 2 so the test stays clean.
   - Easy: *how to solve sudoku, sudoku tips, sudoku for beginners, sudoku strategy, daily sudoku*. Age 35+.
   - Extreme: *hard sudoku, sudoku x wing, advanced sudoku techniques, sudoku chains, expert sudoku*. Age 25+.
7. **Ad → Video ad:** add both YouTube videos for that trailer (9:16 and 16:9). Fill in the copy from the table below, the Orbace square logo (1:1) and a Learn more call to action.
8. **Final URL:** the su-pu page plus paid tracking:
   - Easy: `https://orbacesudoku.com/su-pu/SP-20260924-047554?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=paid_video&utm_campaign=supu_dg&utm_content=easy`
   - Extreme: `https://orbacesudoku.com/su-pu/SP-20260925-683633?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=paid_video&utm_campaign=supu_dg&utm_content=ibtree`
9. **Publish.** Ads usually clear review within a business day.

### Campaign C (Video campaign on Sudoku channels)

1. **New campaign** → objective *YouTube reach, views, and engagements* → **Video** → subtype **Video views**. Name it `SuPu-C-Extreme-Placements`.
2. **Bidding:** Target CPV, $0.05 maximum. **Budget:** $5.00/day.
3. **Networks:** YouTube videos only; uncheck video partners. Same locations and language as A and B.
4. **Ad group → Content → Placements:** search *sudoku* and add 10–20 channels and videos that teach advanced techniques (X-Wing, chains, hardest-puzzle solves). Skip beginner and kids channels.
5. **Ad:** Extreme 16:9, skippable in-stream plus in-feed. Final URL: the Extreme link above, with `utm_campaign=supu_placements`. Headline and call to action from the table below.
