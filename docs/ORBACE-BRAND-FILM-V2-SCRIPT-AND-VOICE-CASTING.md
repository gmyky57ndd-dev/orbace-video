# Orbace Sudoku Brand Film V2 — Launch Script and Voice Casting Brief

**Status:** proposed launch-hero script  
**Target:** 30 seconds  
**Primary master:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps  
**Required derivative:** authored 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps  
**Primary job:** introduce Orbace's category difference and create enough desire to visit the site or download the app.

## 1. Strategic role

This is the first film many people will see. It must not behave like a compressed product tour.

Its job is to establish one memorable distinction:

> Most Sudoku apps remember the result. Orbace remembers the solve.

Replay, Scorecard, Su-Pu, Journal, and competition are evidence supporting that proposition. They are not five unrelated features receiving equal screen time.

The film should leave a cold viewer with three ideas:

1. Orbace preserves the reasoning behind a Sudoku solve.
2. That record can be replayed and studied.
3. Orbace offers a calm path from personal play to fair competition.

## 2. Creative principles

- Open with tension, not a logo.
- Show real current Orbace UI as proof.
- Use one canonical solve whenever the available footage allows it.
- Keep the narration human and conversational.
- Allow the turning point to breathe.
- Use the Journal visual system: warm paper, deep green, crimson seal, restrained gold rules, editorial serif, functional sans-serif, and deliberate motion.
- End with a direct invitation to play/download.
- Do not show a leaderboard, provisional rank, thin beta participation, live competition answer, fabricated interaction, or unrelated Learn technique.
- Do not claim that every solve becomes a shareable Su-Pu. Use product-accurate wording for eligible personal solves.

## 4. Final voiceover script

### Preferred script

> Most Sudoku apps remember the result. Orbace remembers the solve. Every note. Every decision. Every turning point. Rewind the moment the grid opened—and understand why. Your Scorecard shows how you finished. Your Su-Pu preserves how you arrived. Study it. Then, when you're ready, compete. Orbace Sudoku. A calmer way to play. A clearer way to compete.

Approximate length: 57 words.

At approximately 126–132 words per minute, the spoken words occupy roughly 26–27 seconds, leaving room for the turning-point pause and final musical resolve. The editor must time the film to the approved performance rather than mechanically time-stretching the voice.

### Product-safe alternate

Use this if the PM does not want to name Su-Pu in the launch hero:

> Most Sudoku apps remember the result. Orbace remembers the solve. Every note. Every decision. Every turning point. Rewind the moment the grid opened—and understand why. When you finish, Orbace keeps a clear record of how you solved. Study it. Then, when you're ready, compete. Orbace Sudoku. A calmer way to play. A clearer way to compete.

## 5. Voice direction

The desired voice is **quiet intelligence with human warmth**.

It should sound like a thoughtful person sharing a discovery, not an announcer explaining software.

### Performance qualities

- adult, approximately 35–55 in perceived age;
- grounded lower-mid register;
- neutral or lightly regional English accent;
- warm but not breathy;
- measured but not slow or sleepy;
- articulate without sounding theatrical;
- confident without sales pressure;
- small natural smile only on the final Orbace line;
- capable of clean pauses without filling them with affectation.

### Words that need meaning

- `result` — familiar and limited;
- `solve` — the new idea and principal emphasis;
- `turning point` — curiosity, not drama;
- `opened` — quiet recognition;
- `journey` — human, not sentimental;
- `when you're ready` — autonomy, not urgency;
- `Orbace Sudoku` — assured and simple.

### Avoid

- macOS/system narration voices;
- meditation-app whispering;
- luxury perfume-ad delivery;
- over-enunciated corporate explainer tone;
- sports or competition-announcer energy;
- exaggerated British prestige;
- smiling through every sentence;
- vocal fry, heavy breath, or artificial intimacy;
- rushed feature-list cadence;
- a highly recognizable generic social-media AI voice.

## 6. Recommended voice alternatives

### Option A — Directed human actor (recommended final)

Cast a professional actor for the final launch master. Request custom auditions rather than selecting from demo reels alone.

**Primary casting profile**

Warm, grounded woman or gender-neutral voice; perceived age 38–52; neutral North American or lightly international English; lower-mid pitch; intelligent, natural, and conversational.

Why this is the preferred default:

- provides human authority without sounding institutional;
- avoids the increasingly recognizable texture of common AI voices;
- supports emotional nuance around `Orbace remembers the solve`;
- can remain the consistent brand voice for future Journal introductions and major product films.

**Alternate human profile**

Thoughtful man; perceived age 38–55; neutral North American or restrained modern British accent; warm baritone without trailer depth; conversational and lightly scholarly.

Use Voice123, Voices, a local actor, or a direct independent talent relationship. Quote requests must state:

- 30-second online brand film;
- organic website, YouTube, social, PR, and app-store use;
- whether paid social/online advertising is included;
- territories;
- usage term, preferably 12 months with renewal rather than an unnecessary perpetual buyout;
- required pickups;
- whether the talent may become the continuing Orbace brand voice.

Voice rates depend heavily on usage, market, and duration. Do not treat a narration-only quote as paid-ad usage. Voice123 notes that commercial rates are usage-dependent and that experienced US talent commonly begins around several hundred dollars, with broader campaigns costing more.

### Option B — ElevenLabs Professional Voice Clone (best prototype/backup)

Use a paid plan and audition Professional Voice Clones from the Voice Library. ElevenLabs describes Professional Voice Clones as its most consistent production-ready option; paid plans provide commercial usage rights.

Recommended search filters:

- Language: English;
- category: Narration and Advertisement;
- age: Middle Aged;
- gender: audition Female, Neutral, and Male rather than deciding in advance;
- accent: American neutral first, then understated British/international;
- tone keywords: warm, grounded, conversational, intelligent, calm;
- notice period: prefer 12–24 months to reduce continuity risk.

Do not choose a voice simply because it is popular. Common social voices such as Brian, Liam, Adam, Jessica, Matilda, and Sarah may sound familiar to audiences; audition less-saturated professional voices against the Orbace script.

Use AI VO for:

- timing the edit;
- comparing voice profiles;
- early internal and stakeholder review;
- emergency pickup flexibility.

Use it as the final launch voice only after commercial-rights review, a full-film listening test, and comparison against at least three human auditions.

### Option C — Founder or team voice with professional direction

If a founder/team member naturally fits the profile, record them with a remote voice director and proper microphone/environment. This can create the most authentic long-term identity and avoid dependence on a marketplace voice.

Use only if the performance sounds assured and natural. Authenticity does not compensate for unclear diction, poor room sound, or self-conscious delivery.

### Option D — Custom/private professional clone

If Orbace chooses a long-term human actor, negotiate permission to create a private professional clone for pickups and localization. Obtain explicit written consent and usage terms. Never clone an actor, employee, or public figure without informed authorization.

This is a later operational option, not required for launch.

## 7. Audition script

Every candidate must record the same copy:

> Most Sudoku apps remember the result. Orbace remembers the solve. Rewind the moment the grid opened. See what changed—and understand why. And when you're ready, bring your game into fair competition. Orbace Sudoku.

Request three complete interpretations:

1. **Editorial default:** grounded, intimate but not whispered, 134–138 wpm.
2. **Warmer:** slightly more human warmth and curiosity, never sentimental.
3. **More assured:** firmer category confidence, never announcer-like.

For ElevenLabs custom previews, use this 70–150-character excerpt:

> Most Sudoku apps remember the result. Orbace remembers the solve. Rewind the moment the grid opened.

## 8. Audition scoring rubric

Score every audition from 1–5:

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Human credibility | 25% | Does this sound like a real thoughtful person? |
| Brand fit | 20% | Does it feel calm, intelligent, and distinctive? |
| `result/solve` contrast | 15% | Does the central proposition land immediately? |
| Natural pacing | 15% | Can the performance fit 30 seconds with breathing room? |
| Product-name delivery | 10% | Does `Orbace Sudoku` sound natural and memorable? |
| Emotional restraint | 10% | Is it warm without becoming sleepy, luxurious, or theatrical? |
| Technical cleanliness | 5% | Is the recording free from room noise, clicks, distortion, and excessive processing? |

Reject any voice that scores below 4 on human credibility or brand fit, regardless of total score.

## 9. Recording and mix requirements

- Record 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV, mono dry voice.
- No reverb, music, limiting, noise gate artifacts, or baked-in mastering from the actor.
- Capture two full reads plus pickups for every sentence.
- Preserve at least one second of room tone.
- Edit breaths naturally; do not remove every breath.
- Use gentle corrective EQ and compression only.
- Keep music and sound design subordinate to meaning.
- Final target: approximately -14 LUFS integrated, no higher than -1 dBTP.
- Ensure the audio stream spans the full 30-second picture, including the final resolve.

## 11. Approval test

Before final approval, show the film to viewers unfamiliar with Orbace and ask:

> What does Orbace do that seemed different from other Sudoku apps?

Pass if the unaided answer is substantially:

- it remembers how you solved;
- it lets you replay your reasoning;
- it preserves the solve, not only the score.

Fail if the answer is only:

- it is a calm Sudoku app;
- it has lessons;
- it has competition;
- it looks premium.

The brand film succeeds only when the audience remembers the category distinction.
