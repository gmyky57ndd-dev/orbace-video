# Orbace Replay Trailer Standard v1.0

## Purpose
This document is the master creative and production standard for all Orbace Su-Pu replay trailers.

### Core Philosophy
- The replay is the product.
- The trailer is the door.
- Replay the thinking.
- Reveal the turning point.
- Never reveal the entire story.

## Brand Philosophy
Su-Pu preserves human reasoning, not just completed Sudoku boards. Every replay captures decisions, techniques, timing, chapters and turning points.

## Replay Manifesto
Sudoku has always remembered the answer. Orbace believes it should remember the thinking. Every solve is a story; every decision matters; every turning point teaches something.

## Editorial Principles
- Use genuine replay footage only.
- Never fabricate UI, techniques or events.
- Market the replay experience, not individual techniques.
- End with an invitation to the replay, never the solved board.

## Story Framework
1. Hook
2. Rewind
3. Replay begins
4. Turning point
5. Replay resumes
6. Invitation
7. End card

## Visual Language
- Consistent typography and safe areas.
- Real replay timeline, chapter titles and technique labels.
- Slow pushes, freezes and rewinds only.
- No fake overlays or cinematic gimmicks.

## Audio Language
- Warm documentary narration.
- Minimal music supporting the emotional arc.
- Audio profiles: Recognition, Insight, Discovery, Contradiction, Breakthrough, Celebration.

## Replay Language
Standard vocabulary: Replay, Timeline, Turning Point, Chapter, Decision, Technique, Story, Replay the Thinking.

## Creative Pillars
- Human reasoning
- Beautiful logic
- Replay
- Learning
- Calm
- Craftsmanship
- Preservation
- Curiosity

## CTA Standard
Primary CTA: **Watch the complete replay.**

Brand line: **Replay the thinking.**

## Production Standards
Each replay prompt defines timing, narration, captions, audio cues, camera behavior, replay fidelity, safe areas and CTA implementation.

## Delivery Standards
Deliver 9:16 master, 16:9 crop, captions (burned + SRT), thumbnail, title, description, pinned comment and QR variant when appropriate.

## Replay Profiles
### Easy
Recognition → Confidence → Insight → Understanding.

### Medium
Discovery.

### Hard
Strategy.

### Extreme
Curiosity → Tension → Contradiction → Breakthrough.

### Journal
Reflection.

### Competition
Performance.

### Community Challenge
Curiosity → Discussion.

## Marketing Consistency
Every trailer should reinforce:
- Every solve has a story.
- Replay preserves the thinking.
- Curiosity before explanation.
- Understanding before completion.

## Future Redirect Strategy
Current production uses canonical replay URLs. Future campaigns may adopt `/go/<slug>` branded redirects with campaign tracking, but redirect infrastructure is not a prerequisite for trailer production.

## Governance
Replay-specific documents (Easy, Extreme, Journal, Competition, Community) inherit this standard and override only emotional arc, hook, music profile, turning-point treatment and replay-specific timing.
